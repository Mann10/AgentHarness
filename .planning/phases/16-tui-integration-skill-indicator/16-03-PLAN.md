| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-16-08 | Tampering / Spoofing | Footer chip text (tui-ink/src/components/footer.tsx) | accept | Skill names come from author-controlled, Phase-12-validated SKILL.md frontmatter; the chip renders the CANONICAL name from the backend notification payload — the same value already echoed in the system-prompt manifest. The RPC channel is the TUI's own subprocess over pipes (no remote input). Risk LOW and pre-existing (same trust boundary as every other notification payload the TUI renders, e.g. tool_call arguments).
